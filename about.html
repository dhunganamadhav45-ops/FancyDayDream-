Our Business

Fancy Day Dream is a premier fashion destination born right here in Nepal. Our vision is to elevate the local streetwear and fashion scenes by blending world-class aesthetics with the deep-rooted cultural heritage of our country. We don't just sell apparel; we craft experiences that let you express your individual identity. Through dedication, continuous innovation, and a deep understanding of our customer's needs, our business focuses on making premium fashion accessible, stylish, and entirely your own.

Quality Clothes

Our clothes are woven with the exact same threads as some of the most luxurious brands globally. When creating our fabric blends, our number one priority is ensuring a breathable, long-lasting, and exceptionally comfortable fit right out of the box. Whether it is adding custom Nepali Dhaka patterns, utilizing premium cotton for our hoodies, or perfecting the stitch counts to prevent tear, you will physically feel the difference when you wear our clothes. Our Story

The story of Fancy Day Dream began with a simple question: why do Nepali youth have to look outside the borders for clothing that resonates with modern global aesthetics? Started by a small group of ambitious local tailors and visionary designers in Kathmandu, we took a leap of faith to bring superior craftsmanship home. Through late-night design sessions, testing hundreds of fabrics, and building our own digital studio ground-up, we are proud to say that we have successfully written the next chapter of local apparel.
